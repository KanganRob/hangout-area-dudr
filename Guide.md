# Guide

## Stats Guide
Each character in the campaign has specific **abilities** and **combat** stats which control how strong they are and also how they function in battle.

The tables below provide a run-down on the **different stats** and **damage types** currently available within the campaign.

| Stat | Description | Example
|-|-|-|
| Health | The amount of damage that the unit can take before it is knocked **unconscious**. | 554 HP
| Mana | The main source for casting abilities. If you don't have enough mana to use an ability, you can't cast it. | 297 Mana
| ATK | Short for **attack**; the value used to determine the overall strength of your basic attacks, and some abilities. | 143 ATK
| Armour | Reduces a percentage of any **physical** damage that you would take. Has diminishing returns. | 65 Armour
| Magic Resist | Reduces a percentage of any **magical** damage that you would take. Has diminishing returns. | 42 Magic Resist 
| Health Regeneration | The rate at which you recover any **missing health**, per second. | 1.8 HP/sec
| Mana Regeneration | The rate at which you recover any **missing mana**, per second. | 2.4 Mana/sec
| HP Increase | The amount of additional **max health** you get when you level up. | +82 HP/level
| Mana Increase | The amount of additional **max mana** you get when you level up. | +29 Mana/level
| ATK Increase | The amount of additional **ATK** you get when you level up. | +4 ATK/level
| ARM Increase | The amount of additional **Armour** you get when you level up. | +3 Armour/level
| MR Increase | The amount of additional **Magic Resist** you get when you level up. | +1.7 Magic Resist/level
| REG Increase | The amount of additional **health and mana regeneration** you get when you level up. | +0.3 HP regen & +0.1 Mana regen per level
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*Thanks to various other games with similar combat systems for inspiration on these stats.*

Note that each stat varies for each character, usually based on their class. Some characters have **higher base health and a lower base ATK** value, while others may have **lower base health and thus a higher base ATK** value.

## Damage Types
There are **currently only 3 damage types available** in the game, as follows:

| Damage Type | Amplifier | Counter | Source |
|-|-|-|-|
| Physical | ATK | Armour | Most basic attacks and some character abilities or item passives.
| Magical | Ability Power (AP) | Magic Resist | Most character abilities and some item passives.
| True | None | Max Health | Some abilities and item passives.
-----------------------------------------------------------------
*You may notice that these are also prevalent in many other games, so again, thanks to those for the inspiration for these damage types.*

## Levelling Up
Okay, so we've now covered the **character stats** and the **damage types** available in-game, which is great.

However, keen eyes would've already noticed that **several stats increase each time the character levels up**. So, how does a character level up, and what actually happens when they do?

### Character XP
I'm sure you already expected this to be the main source of levelling up. You can gain XP in various ways, from:
- Completing quests
- Defeating enemies or bosses in battle
- Looting dungeons
- Opening chests throughout the open world

to a few others that I'm going to let you find out for yourself.

When your character **levels up**, the gain their *per level increase* to each of the main stats as normal AND they gain a **Stat Point**. What is a **Stat Point**? Hold on, I'm getting there.

### Stat Points
**Stat Points** are used to improve __one__ of your character's out-of-combat stats, similar to the **S.P.E.C.I.A.L** system found in the Fallout universe -- wait, no, it's basically that system. Whoops.

Anyway, we're getting side-tracked here. Here's a list of the stats, what they do and the maximum bonus you can get for that stat when it reaches its **max investment** (10 points).

| Stat | Max Value | Function | Cap | 
|-|-|-|-|
| Agility | 10 | Increases your character's movement speed by +5% per point. | +50% movement speed
| Charisma | 10 | Increases NPC trust rate by +5% per point. | +50% NPC trust rate
| Endurance | 10 | Increases your maximum backpack size by +1 and weight by +20 per point. | 18 slots, 350 carry weight
| Intelligence | 10 | Increases XP gains by 2% per point. | +20% XP gain
| Luck | 10 | Increases the chance of getting better loot drops by +5%. | +50% better loot drops
| Perception | 10 | Increases your critical-hit chance by +5% per point. | +50% critical-hit chance
| Strength | 10 | Increases your damage multiplier by +5% per point. | +50% damage multiplier
-------------------------------------------------------
*Some items may allow you to exceed these limits by granting you extra points in certain stats. Each extra point still grants the same boost -- as if you'd invested an extra point in that stat.*

That's sort of true, but there are some **milestone levels** where you'll get **more than 1 Stat Point** when you reach it.
